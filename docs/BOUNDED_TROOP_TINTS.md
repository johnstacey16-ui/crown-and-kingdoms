# Bounded native troop tint cache — 29 October 2026

Animated troop palettes now recolour only the requested original-resolution frame, rather than retaining a full transparent atlas copy for each kingdom. One global least-recently-used cache retains at most 33,554,432 canvas pixels (128 MiB of nominal RGBA storage). Entries are keyed by source-image identity, kingdom, palette and crop rectangle. A reload or changed palette cannot reuse stale colours. Shared crops retain each animation's own dimensions, scale and foot anchor. Oversized frames remain drawable without entering the retained cache.

The troop painter directly reuses a prepared native crop, avoiding its former second texture copy. Buildings, base-pose fallbacks, neutral environment/resources and external fractional-frame packs retain their established full-sheet/source sampling.
